Patterns that cut across layers

These are the parts of the story that are most likely to repeat in the next wave.

Three questions to ask of any layer

Most of the outcomes above follow from the answers to these.

Is someone building ahead of demand with borrowed money?

Debt-funded capacity built on growth forecasts ends in bankruptcy when growth slows, even if demand catches up later (L1, L3).

Where is the bottleneck, and who owns it?

Control follows the bottleneck. When it moved from software to the physical line, AOL lost the customer to the cable and phone companies (L4).

Does the idea need something that isn't ready yet?

Grocery delivery, pet supplies online and music streaming were right ideas that died waiting for broadband, logistics and payments (L7, L8).
