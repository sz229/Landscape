Patterns that cut across layers

These are the parts of the story that are most likely to repeat in the next wave.

Three questions to ask of any layer

Most of the outcomes above follow from the answers to these.

How much capital does it take to compete?

Heavy, ongoing capital spending leaves room for only two or three winners (L1, L4). Light layers stay crowded (L7, L8).

Can open source make it free?

If yes, the layer becomes a standard and the value moves to whoever runs it as a service or sells support (L3, L5).

How hard is it for a customer to leave?

Data and daily workflows hold customers in place (L6, L8). Raw compute does not, so it competes on scale and price (L4).
